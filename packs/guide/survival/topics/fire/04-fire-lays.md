# Tepee and lean-to
![Fire lays: tepee, lean-to, cross-ditch, pyramid](atp-p125-1)

- **Tepee:** lean kindling in a cone over the tinder and light the bottom. Quick and hot. Burns even damp wood.
- **Lean-to:** push a green stick into the ground at about 30 degrees over the tinder. Lean kindling against it, then light the tinder.

# Log cabin, pyramid and star

These lays burn longer. Start each one with a small tepee or a bed of coals.

- **Log cabin:** stack pairs of sticks in crossed layers around a small tepee. It burns bright and hot and soon gives coals for cooking or a signal fire.
- **Pyramid:** lay two logs side by side, then a solid layer of smaller logs across them. Add three or four more layers, each smaller and at right angles to the one below. Light a small fire on top. It burns downward slowly and needs little feeding.
- **Star:** lay four to six logs like the spokes of a wheel, ends meeting in the fire. Push them in as the ends burn. Saves fuel and uses logs too big to break. Tend it constantly.
- **Cross-ditch:** scrape an X about 12 in (30 cm) across and 3 in (7.5 cm) deep. Tinder goes in the middle, kindling over it; air flows in underneath.

NOTE: Watch every fire, even a slow one. Never leave it alone.
