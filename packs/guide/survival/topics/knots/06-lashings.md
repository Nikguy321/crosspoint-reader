# Square lashing
![Square lashing in four steps](atp-p211-1)

Joins poles that cross at right angles.

1. Clove hitch on the upright, just below the crosspiece.
2. Wrap over the crosspiece and behind the upright, 3 or 4 times. Keep it tight.
3. Make 2 or 3 frapping turns between the poles, around the wraps. Pull hard.
4. Finish with a clove hitch on the crosspiece.
+++
# Diagonal lashing
![Diagonal lashing in three steps](atp-p212-1)

For poles that cross at an angle or spring apart.

1. Clove hitch around both poles where they cross.
2. Make 3 turns side by side, diagonally around the crossing.
3. Make 3 more along the other diagonal.
4. Make 2 frapping turns between the poles.
5. Finish with a clove hitch on a pole.
+++
# Shear lashing
![Shear lashing on side-by-side poles](atp-p213-1)

Binds 2 or 3 poles side by side for A-frames and tripods.

1. Clove hitch on an outer pole.
2. Make 7 or 8 loose turns around all the poles.
3. Make frapping turns between each pair of poles.
4. Finish with a clove hitch on the far outer pole.
5. Spread the legs.

Load-test any frame before you sleep under it.
