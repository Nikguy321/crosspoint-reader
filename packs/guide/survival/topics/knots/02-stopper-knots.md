# Overhand knot
![Tying an overhand knot](atp-p197-1)

The simplest knot.

1. Make a loop near the end.
2. Pass the end through the loop.
3. Pull tight.

Use it to stop an end fraying or slipping through a hole, and to back up other knots. It can jam hard and roughly halves the rope's strength.

# Figure-eight knot
![Tying a figure-eight knot](atp-p199-1)

A bigger stopper that is easier to untie after a hard pull.

1. Make a loop near the end.
2. Take the end around behind the standing part.
3. Bring it back down through the loop.
4. Pull tight and dress it into a neat 8.
