# Square knot
![Tying a square knot](atp-p200-1)

1. Right end over left, and under.
2. Left end over right, and under.
3. Pull tight. Each end should lie beside its own rope.

If the ends stick out crosswise it is a granny knot and will slip. Use it for bundles and bandage ties.

NOTE: Not for joining ropes under heavy load. Use a sheet bend.
+++
# Sheet bend
![Tying a single sheet bend](atp-p201-1)

Joins two dry ropes, even of different sizes.

1. Make a bight in the thicker rope.
2. Pass the thin end up through the bight.
3. Take it around behind both sides of the bight.
4. Tuck it under itself, not through the bight.
5. Pull tight.

It can loosen when the load comes off. For wet rope use the double sheet bend.
+++
# Double sheet bend
![Tying a double sheet bend](atp-p201-2)

For wet or slippery ropes, or ropes of very different sizes. It holds better under heavy load.

1. Tie a sheet bend but do not tighten it.
2. Take the thin end around the bight a second time.
3. Tuck it under itself again.
4. Pull tight.
