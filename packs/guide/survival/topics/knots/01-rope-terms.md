# Rope words
![Parts of a rope](atp-p196-1)

- **Running end**: the free end you tie with.
- **Standing part**: the rest of the rope.
- **Bight**: a U-bend; **loop**: the rope crosses itself.
- **Turn**: around a post and back the way it came; **round turn**: fully around it.

**Dress** every knot (no twists, parts lying neat), then **set** it: pull every part tight. Leave a tail about 4 in (10 cm).
