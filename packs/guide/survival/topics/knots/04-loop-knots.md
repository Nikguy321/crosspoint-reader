# Bowline
![Tying a bowline](atp-p202-1)

A fixed loop that holds under load. The rabbit comes up the hole, around the tree, back down.

1. Make a small loop, end side on top.
2. Bring the end up through it.
3. Take it behind the standing part.
4. Pass it back down the small loop.
5. Pull the standing part to set it.

It can shake loose when slack. Tie off the tail with an overhand.

# Bowline on a bight
![Tying a bowline on a bight](atp-p203-1)

Two fixed loops in the middle of a rope.

1. Double the rope and make the small loop of a bowline with it.
2. Push the bight up through the small loop.
3. Open the bight and pass it down over the two big loops.
4. Pull the big loops to set it.

NOTE: Not climbing training. Never hang a person's weight on a knot unless you are trained.
