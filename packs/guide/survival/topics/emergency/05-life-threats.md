# Life threats first

1. **Make it safe.** Watch for danger to you and them. Move them only if staying is more dangerous.
2. **Call 911** or send an SOS now, or have someone do it.
3. **Stop severe bleeding.** Press hard on the wound with cloth or a gloved hand until help takes over.
4. **Tourniquet** if an arm or leg still bleeds heavily: high on the limb, above the wound, not on a joint. Tighten until bleeding stops; it hurts. Note the time. Do not take it off.
5. **Check breathing.** Not responding, and not breathing or only gasping? Start CPR: push hard and fast on the center of the chest, about 2 in (5 cm) deep, 100 to 120 a minute.
6. **Breathing but not awake?** Roll them onto their side, keeping head and neck in line.
7. **Keep them warm.** Insulate them from the ground, cover them, talk calmly.
