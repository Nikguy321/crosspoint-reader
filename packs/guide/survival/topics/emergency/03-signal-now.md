# Signal for rescue now

**Three of anything means "help".** Three whistle blasts, three flashes, three fires. Rest, then repeat.

- **Whistle:** three blasts, pause, three more. It carries farther than shouting and saves strength.
- **Bright colors:** spread clothing or gear in the nearest open space.
- **Mirror:** flash a mirror or shiny object toward an aircraft or its sound. Sweep the horizon even when none is in sight.
- **Ground sign:** a big **V** (need help) or **X** (need medical help) of rocks, logs or gear, each line at least 3 ft (1 m) wide and 20 ft (6 m) long.
- **At night:** flash a light in SOS: three short, three long, three short.
- **Fires:** three in a triangle or a line, about 80 ft (25 m) apart, or one if you are alone.

WARNING: No fire when fire danger is high, in a fire ban or in wildfire season: a wildfire can trap you and others. Otherwise clear to bare soil; never leave it.
