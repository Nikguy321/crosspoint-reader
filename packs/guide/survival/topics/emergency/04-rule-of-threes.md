# The rule of threes

A simple way to decide what to fix first. A person can last roughly:

- **3 minutes** without air, or with severe bleeding
- **3 hours** exposed to cold, wet, windy or very hot weather
- **3 days** without water
- **3 weeks** without food

Work through them in that order:

1. **Safety first.** Get away from danger. Stop severe bleeding. Make sure everyone can breathe.
2. **Shelter and warmth.** Get out of wind and rain. Get dry. Get off the cold ground.
3. **Water.** Drink before you are thirsty; in the cold you feel thirst less. Heat, hard work and cold air all use water fast. Treat wild water when you can.
4. **Food last.** Hunger feels bad but is rarely what kills. Never eat unknown plants or wild mushrooms.

NOTE: These numbers are a rule of thumb, not a timer. Wet cold or hard heat can kill in less than 3 hours; a child, an injury or an illness shortens every time. Act early.
