# Has the pilot seen you?
![Aircraft acknowledgments: wings rocking; right-hand circle](fm92-p310-1)

A pilot who has seen you may fly low, flash the landing lights or **rock the wings**: message understood.

A full **right-hand circle** means seen but not understood. Make your signal clearer. No sign at all? Assume you were not seen.

Keep signaling until you are sure. Then stay put; rescue may take hours.

# Helicopter safety

- **Before it lands,** pack away or weigh down everything loose: tarps, clothing, sleeping bags. Rotor wind can throw them into the blades.
- **Protect your eyes.** Wear glasses or shield your eyes with a hand. Blinded by dust? Crouch or sit down and wait.
- **Stay where you are** until the crew signals you. Often a crew member walks to you.
- **When signaled, approach from the front or side,** where the pilot can see you. Walk, do not run, and keep low.
- **On a slope, approach from the downhill side.** The blades are closer to the ground uphill.
- **Never go near the tail.** The tail rotor spins almost invisibly and can kill.
- **Hold on to your hat.** Never reach up for or chase anything that blows away.
- **Carry gear low,** never above your shoulders.
- **If a cable or seat is lowered,** let it touch the ground before you touch it.

Do exactly what the crew tells you.
