# Aim a mirror
![Aiming a mirror with two fingers](fm92-p304-1)

Use a mirror or any shiny thing: a can lid, foil, polished metal.

1. Hold your other hand at arm's length. Make a V with two fingers around the aircraft.
2. Flash sunlight onto your fingers.
3. Tilt the mirror so the light passes through the V. Rock it slowly across the target.

Aircraft high overhead? Lie on your back, as shown. Can't see it? Flash toward the sound.

# Aim using a stake
![Aiming at the top of a stake](fm92-p305-1)

1. Plant a 4 to 5 foot (1.2 to 1.5 m) stick.
2. Move until its top lines up with the target.
3. Tilt the mirror until the light hits the stick top.

Sweep the horizon even with no aircraft seen. In ideal conditions a flash can show 100 miles (160 km) away.

WARNING: Never hold the beam on a cockpit over a few seconds; it can dazzle the pilot.
