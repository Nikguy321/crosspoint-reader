# Turn around, don't drown

**Never walk or drive into moving flood water.** Just 6 inches (15 cm) of fast water can knock you down; 12 inches (30 cm) carries away most cars.

**It does not have to rain on you.** A storm miles upstream can send a wall of water down a dry wash under blue sky.

- **Camp high**, above the high-water marks: lines of drift or mud on banks and trees. Never camp in a dry wash or creek bed.
- **Leave canyons and creek beds** when storms threaten upstream.
- Burned slopes flood and slide more easily for years.

**Warning signs:** thunder or building clouds upstream, water turning muddy, floating sticks, rising water or a stronger current, a dry wash starting to flow, a roar from upstream.

1. **Climb to high ground at once.** Water can rise in minutes or seconds.
2. **Leave your gear.**
3. **Do not try to cross.** See **Crossing streams**.
4. **Stay high and wait.** It can take hours for the water to drop.
