# Rules first

NOTE: Fishing needs a license and follows local rules on seasons, limits and gear. Set lines, nets, traps and spears are banned in many places outside a true survival emergency.

In a real emergency, getting home safe comes first. Even then:

- **Take only what you need** to eat now.
- **Fish from shore.** Do not wade into fast or cold water, or walk onto ice, for a fish.
- **Skip water that smells bad or has scum, foam or colored streaks.** Its fish can hold toxins that cooking does not remove.
- **Remove every line and trap** when you leave, so they do not keep killing or snag people and animals.
- **Cook all fish** all the way through.

**Where fish are:** eddies behind rocks and logs, deep pools, shade under banks and brush, and around sunken logs and weed beds.

**When:** early morning and evening, and just before a storm.

Bait with what fish eat there: worms, insects, or the guts of an earlier catch.

# Improvised hooks and gorges
![Improvised hooks: gorge, wire, thorn and carved wood](atp-p108-1)

Make hooks from wire, a pin, a thorn lashed to a stick, or a carved wood shank with a bone or wire point tied in a notch.

**Gorge:** a sliver of wood or bone, sharp at both ends, tied at the middle. Bury it lengthwise in bait. Let the fish swallow it deep; when the line tightens, it turns crosswise and lodges. Do not jerk it.
+++
# Set lines and traps
![Basket, pool and tidal fish traps](atp-p110-2)

These work while you rest.

**Stakeout:** tie a line between two stakes just under the surface. Hang two short baited lines from it that cannot tangle.

**Stake trap:** in calm, shallow water, drive stakes in a pen with a narrow funnel entrance. Fish swim in and miss the way out.

Check them often, and watch the tide on beaches. Remove them all when you leave.
