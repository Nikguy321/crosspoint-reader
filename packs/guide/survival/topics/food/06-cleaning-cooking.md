# Clean fish

Fish spoil fast, especially in warm weather. Clean them soon after you catch them.

1. Cut out the gills.
2. Slit the belly from the vent to the throat and pull out the guts. Scrape out the dark blood line along the spine.
3. Scale the fish, or skin it.
4. Rinse it in clean water.

**Throw it away if it has:** sunken eyes, an odd smell, pale or faded gills, a sticky, slimy feel instead of a wet one, or flesh that stays dented when pressed. Cooking will not make spoiled fish safe.

**Cook all fish all the way through,** until the flesh turns opaque and flakes apart, or 145 F (63 C) on a thermometer.

Use guts as bait, or get rid of them far from camp and trails, as local rules say. Near camp they draw bears and other animals.
+++
# Clean small game

- **Wear gloves,** or a plastic bag over your hand. Rabbits, hares and rodents can carry tularemia, a serious infection.
- **Leave alone** any animal that looked sick, acted strangely or was found dead.
- **Work away from camp** and well away from your water source.
- **Do not eat or drink** while you work.

To clean a rabbit-sized animal:

1. Cut the skin around the back legs and peel it down off the body, like pulling off a sweater. Cut off the head and feet.
2. Slit the belly skin carefully, without cutting into the guts.
3. Pull out all the insides, including the chest.
4. If the meat or organs look or smell wrong, throw it all away.

Keep raw meat away from other food. Wash your hands and knife with soap and clean water afterward.
+++
# Cook it through
![Tin-can stove and pot hung from a crane](fm92-p234-1)

**Boil** meat when you can and drink the broth; it keeps the most food value.

Cook game to 160 F (71 C) and birds to 165 F (74 C): steaming hot all through, no pink.

WARNING: Drying or smoking alone does not kill parasites. Cook it first.

WARNING: Never cook inside a tent or closed shelter. Obey fire bans. See FIRE.
