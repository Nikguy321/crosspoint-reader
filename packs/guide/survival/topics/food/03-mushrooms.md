# Never eat wild mushrooms

In a survival situation, **do not eat any wild mushroom.** They have almost no food value, and deadly kinds look like edible ones. Even experts can find them hard to tell apart.

**The death cap** was brought to North America and is spreading along the West Coast. It looks like an ordinary mushroom. Cooking does not destroy its poison.

Symptoms start late, about 6 to 24 hours after eating: stomach pain, vomiting and diarrhea. The person may then seem to get better while the liver is failing. Early hospital care saves lives.

See **Death cap mushroom** in PACIFIC NORTHWEST.

WARNING: If anyone ate a wild mushroom, do not wait for symptoms. Call 911 or send an SOS now. With signal, call Poison Help: 1-800-222-1222. Keep any leftover mushroom to show.
