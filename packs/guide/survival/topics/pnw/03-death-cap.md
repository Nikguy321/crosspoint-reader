# Death cap

An introduced mushroom, spreading along the West Coast in parks, yards and woods, often under oaks and other broadleaf trees. It causes most mushroom deaths worldwide. **One mushroom can kill an adult.**

- It can look like edible mushrooms. Even experienced foragers are fooled.
- Cooking, drying or freezing does not destroy the poison.
- **Symptoms are delayed,** usually 6 to 24 hours: stomach pain, vomiting, diarrhea. They may then seem better while the liver fails.

**If anyone may have eaten a wild mushroom:**

1. Call 911 or send an SOS now. With signal, also call Poison Help, 1-800-222-1222.
2. Do not wait for symptoms: early hospital care saves lives. Keep leftovers to show.

WARNING: Never eat a wild mushroom. Keep children and pets away from them.
