# Avoid cougars

Cougars, also called mountain lions, live in forests and hills across the region. Most try to avoid people.

- **Do not hike or jog alone.** Go in groups, with adults watching children.
- **Keep children close** and in sight. Do not let them play alone, especially at dawn and dusk.
- Keep pets close. Do not leave them outside at night.
- Be most alert at **dawn and dusk.**
- **Never approach a cougar.** Leave it room to get away.

+++

# If you meet a cougar

1. **Do not run.** Running can make it chase you.
2. **Stand and face it.** Make eye contact.
3. **Do not crouch or bend over.** A standing person does not look like prey.
4. Pick up small children so they do not run. Do it without bending over or turning away.
5. **Look bigger:** raise your arms, open your jacket.
6. Wave your arms slowly and speak firmly in a loud voice.
7. Give it a clear way to escape.
8. If it comes closer, throw stones or branches you can reach without crouching or turning your back.

**If it attacks, fight back** with rocks, sticks, a jacket, anything you have. A cougar goes for the head and neck, so try to stay on your feet and face it.
