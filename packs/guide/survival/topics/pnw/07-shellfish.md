# Toxic shellfish

Clams, mussels, oysters, scallops and other shellfish can carry natural poisons from algae blooms in the sea. On this coast, **paralytic shellfish poisoning (PSP)** and **domoic acid** show up almost every year.

- You cannot see, smell or taste the poison. Toxic shellfish look and taste normal.
- **Cooking, freezing, canning, salting or smoking does not destroy it.**
- Shellfish sold in shops are tested. The danger is shellfish you gather yourself.

**Before you gather any:**

- Check the **official closure line** or notice for that exact beach, on the day you harvest.
- Never take shellfish from a closed beach.
- Take only shellfish that stay covered by water at high tide.
- Take only live ones: clams, mussels and oysters close when tapped. Throw out cracked ones and any that do not open when cooked.
- Cook shellfish well. It kills germs, but **not** the algae poisons.

# Shellfish poisoning signs

**Paralytic poisoning (PSP):** numbness and tingling of the lips, tongue and face, then the arms and legs, usually 30 to 60 minutes after eating. It can spread to paralysis and stop breathing.

**Domoic acid:** vomiting, stomach pain and diarrhea within 24 hours, then headache, confusion and memory loss.

**If anyone feels tingling or numbness after eating shellfish:**

1. **Call 911 or send an SOS at once.** Do not wait to see if it passes.
2. With signal, also call Poison Help, 1-800-222-1222.
3. Keep the person still and watch their breathing.
4. If breathing stops, start CPR, with rescue breaths if you are trained. Keep going (FIRST AID, CPR and choking).
5. Tell others who ate the same meal to get checked.

WARNING: No home remedy works and there is no antidote. Hospital breathing support saves lives.
