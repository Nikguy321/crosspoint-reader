# Avoid bears

Black bears live in forests across the region. Most bears avoid people if they hear them coming.

- **Make noise** in thick brush, near loud streams and in berry patches. Talk, call out, clap.
- Travel in groups. Groups are noisier and seem bigger.
- **Never let a bear get your food.** Keep food, trash and anything scented packed away and out of reach, and away from where you sleep.
- Never approach a bear or cubs.
- Carry bear spray where it is allowed. Keep it on your belt or chest strap, not in your pack, and learn how to use it before you go.

WARNING: Bear spray is not a repellent. Never spray it on gear, tents or skin. Use it only on a bear coming at you.

+++

# Meeting a black bear

1. **Stay calm. Do not run.** Bears chase running animals.
2. **Do not climb a tree.** Bears climb well.
3. Stand still and talk calmly so it knows you are a person.
4. Pick up small children right away, without shouting.
5. Look bigger: slowly wave your arms above your head.
6. Move away slowly and sideways, watching the bear.
7. Keep your pack on.

**If a black bear attacks:** do NOT play dead. Get to a car or building if you can. If not, **fight back hard** with anything: rocks, branches, bear spray. Aim kicks and blows at its face and muzzle.

WARNING: If any bear attacks you in your tent, or stalks you and then attacks, fight back, whatever kind of bear it is. This is rare, but it may see you as food.

+++

# Grizzly bears

Grizzlies (brown bears) are rare in this region but can turn up in remote mountains. Color will not tell you: black bears can be brown or blond. A grizzly has a **shoulder hump**, short round ears and a dished face.

**If a grizzly attacks after you surprised it:**

1. Use bear spray if you can.
2. **Play dead:** lie flat on your stomach, hands clasped behind your neck.
3. Spread your legs so it is hard to roll you over.
4. Keep your pack on. It protects your back.
5. Stay still until you are sure it has gone, several minutes at least.
6. **If the attack keeps going, fight back** with everything you have.

NOTE: Play dead only for a grizzly defending itself, its cubs or its food after a surprise meeting. Never play dead with a black bear.
