# Sprains

A sprain is a stretched or torn joint, often an ankle, knee or wrist.

1. **Rest it.** Stop and take the weight off.
2. **Cold:** a bag of ice and water, or cloth soaked in a cold stream. Put a thin cloth between it and the skin. Up to 20 minutes at a time, several times a day.
3. **Light compression:** a snug, not tight, wrap. Loosen it if fingers or toes go numb, tingle, or turn pale or blue.
4. **Raise it** above the heart when resting.
5. **Must walk out?** Tape or splint the joint for support, use a walking stick, and go slowly.

Get medical care if the person cannot put weight on it, the limb looks bent or out of place, it is numb, or it is not getting better. A joint that looks out of place needs care right away.

NOTE: Reference only - not a substitute for first-aid training. Call for help first.

# Splint a broken bone

![Padded board splint tied above and below a break](atp-p46-1)

Splint it as you find it; do not straighten it. Pad well and make the splint reach past the joints above and below. Tie above and below the break, never over it. Check that fingers or toes stay warm and pink; if not, loosen the ties. Bone through the skin? Cover it; do not push it back.

NOTE: Reference only - not a substitute for first-aid training. Call for help first.

# Slings

A sling holds a hurt arm, wrist or collarbone still with the forearm across the chest, hand a little higher than the elbow.

- **Cloth sling:** fold a large square of cloth into a triangle. Put the forearm in it and tie the two ends behind the neck. Pad the knot.
- **Shirt tail:** fold the bottom of the person's own shirt up over the forearm and pin or tie it to the shirt above.
- **Belt or strap:** make a loop around the neck and wrist.

Then tie a wide band of cloth around the arm and chest so the arm cannot swing.

Check often: fingers warm, pink and with feeling. If not, loosen the ties.

NOTE: Reference only - not a substitute for first-aid training. Call for help first.
