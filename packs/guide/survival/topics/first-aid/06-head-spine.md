# Head and spine injuries

After a fall or a blow to the head:

1. **Do not move the person** unless they are in danger where they lie: rockfall, water, fire.
2. **Ask them to keep as still as possible**, head and neck too. No collars.
3. **Call 911 or send an SOS.** Say it is a head or neck injury.
4. If you must move or roll them, use helpers: head, neck and body together, as one piece.
5. Keep them warm; slide insulation under them without twisting them. Stay with them and watch their breathing.

**Get help urgently** if you see any of these:

- worsening headache, or repeated vomiting
- confusion, slurred speech, odd behavior
- one pupil bigger than the other
- very sleepy or cannot be woken
- seizure, weakness, numbness or poor balance
- neck or back pain, or cannot move a limb

NOTE: Reference only - not a substitute for first-aid training. Call for help first.
