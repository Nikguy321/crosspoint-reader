# Check the scene and the person

1. **Is it safe for you?** Look for falling rock, moving water, fire, lightning, traffic or animals. Do not become a second patient.
2. **Move the person only if they are in danger** where they lie.
3. **Tap and shout.** Tap their shoulder and shout, "Are you OK?"
4. **Check breathing** for no more than 10 seconds. Watch the chest rise and fall. Gasping is not normal breathing.
5. **Call 911 or send an SOS now**, or point at one person and tell them to do it. Say where you are first.
6. **Not breathing normally?** Start CPR right away (next topic).
7. **Breathing?** Look for heavy bleeding and stop it. If they do not respond, use the recovery position (next page).

If they are awake, say your name, ask how you can help, and tell them help is coming. Let them sit or lie however is most comfortable.

NOTE: Reference only - not a substitute for first-aid training. Call for help first.

# Recovery position

Use it when the person is **breathing but not responding** and you do not think the neck or back is hurt. Lying on the side keeps the airway clear if they vomit.

1. Kneel beside them. Lay the arm nearest you out at a right angle.
2. Bring the far arm across the chest. Hold the back of that hand against their near cheek.
3. Bend the far knee up, foot flat on the ground.
4. Pull on the far knee to roll them toward you onto their side.
5. Tilt the head back a little so the mouth points down and can drain.
6. Put insulation under them and cover them. Keep them warm.
7. Watch their breathing the whole time. If it stops, roll them onto their back and start CPR.

Neck or back injury and they vomit? Roll them as one piece, with a helper keeping the head in line with the body.

NOTE: Reference only - not a substitute for first-aid training. Call for help first.
