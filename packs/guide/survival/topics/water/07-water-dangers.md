# Water you must not drink

Boiling, bleach and camp filters do not make these safe.

WARNING: Do NOT drink seawater, even a little. The salt makes you lose more water than you drank. <!-- myth-ok -->

WARNING: Do NOT drink urine, blood or alcohol. They make dehydration worse or make you sick. <!-- myth-ok -->

- **Chemicals.** Water below mines, factories, dumps, roads or farm fields, or near fuel spills. Fuel and toxic chemicals cannot be boiled or filtered out.
- **Algae blooms.** Water with foam, scum, mats or a paint-like film, or colored bright green, blue, brown or red. Boiling does not remove the toxins and can concentrate them. Stay out, and keep dogs out too.
- **Warning signs.** An oily sheen, chemical smell, dead fish or animals, or no healthy plants around the water.
- **Stagnant pools** that smell bad or look discolored. Choose flowing water.

If seawater is all you have, a ground solar still can make a small amount of fresh water from it. See **Solar and bag stills**.
