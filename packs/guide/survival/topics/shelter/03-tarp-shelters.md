# Tarp lean-to
![Tarp lean-to, back to the wind](atp-p140-1)

1. Find two trees 7-10 ft (2-3 m) apart. Check the wind.
2. Tie a line from each top corner to a tree, waist to chest high.
3. Stake the bottom edge down, back to the wind.
4. Tie a short stick to each line near the tarp so rain drips off, not inside.
5. Pile brush at the sides and leaves inside.
+++
# Tarp tent
![Tarp tent on a line between two trees](atp-p141-1)

A tent sheds rain and wind on both sides but has less room.

1. Tie a line between two trees 7-10 ft (2-3 m) apart, about knee high. Drape the tarp over it.
2. Pull one side tight and stake it.
3. Stake the other side the same way.
+++
# Center support and staking
![Tarp tent with a pole support outside](atp-p141-2)

If the middle sags, tie it up to a branch above, or prop it with a pole or two poles crossed outside.

Keep the cloth tight and steep. Don't touch it in rain: it drips where touched.

Stakes: 1 in by 12 in (2.5 by 30 cm), angled away from the pull. Rocky ground: tie to a heavy rock. Sand: bury a tied stick.
