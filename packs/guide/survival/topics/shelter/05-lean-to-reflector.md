# Build the lean-to
![Lean-to with a log reflector wall in front](atp-p146-1)

1. Tie a crossbar between two trees 7 ft (2 m) apart, waist to chest high.
2. Lean 5-8 poles 10 ft (3 m) long against it, back to the wind.
3. Weave saplings across them.
4. Shingle with boughs or brush from the bottom up.
5. Put a thick bed inside.
+++
# Reflector wall and fire

A wall of green logs on the far side of the fire throws its heat into the open side of the lean-to.

1. Drive four stakes about 5 ft (1.5 m) long into the ground in two pairs, a little apart.
2. Stack green logs between each pair to make two log walls.
3. Fill the gap between the walls with dirt. This makes the wall stronger and reflects more heat.
4. Tie the tops of the stakes together so the wall stays up.

Keep the fire **small** and about 3 ft (1 m) in front of the opening, well clear of the roof edge, boughs and bedding. Clear the ground to bare soil around it. Never leave it burning while you sleep unless someone stays awake to watch it.

NOTE: Poles laid from the crossbar to the top of the wall make a drying rack for wet clothes.

WARNING: Check fire restrictions before any trip. See the FIRE section for fire safety.
