# How you lose heat
![How the body loses heat](atp-p50-1)

Your body loses heat five ways:

- **Radiation:** from bare skin, head, neck and hands.
- **Conduction:** into cold ground, rock or snow you touch.
- **Convection:** wind strips the warm air off you.
- **Evaporation:** sweat and wet clothes.
- **Breathing:** cold air in, warm air out.

Clothing and shelter block each one.
+++
# Small, early, insulated

Build your shelter while you still have daylight and energy. Start by mid-afternoon. A shelter built in the dark, when you are tired and cold, is a poor one.

- **Use what is there.** A tarp, a fallen tree or a rock overhang is faster than building from scratch.
- **Small is warm.** Make it just big enough to sit up and lie down in, with your gear. A big space steals your body heat.
- **Ground first.** Cold ground drains heat faster than cold air. Put a thick layer of insulation under you before you add more on top.
- **Stay dry.** Wet clothing pulls heat away many times faster than dry clothing.
- **Don't sweat while you build.** Open your jacket or take off a layer while working, and put it back on when you stop.

WARNING: Cold can kill even above 40 F (4 C) when you are wet from rain, sweat or cold water.
